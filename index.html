Welcome Message

FSS Oyo: The Pioneer
Geoinformatics Education in
Nigeria

I welcome you to Federal School of Surveying, Oyo — the beacon
of Surveying and Geoinformatics education in Nigeria and Sub-
Saharan Africa. Here, professionals and technologists in the field
of Land Surveying, Geoinformatics, and other related programmes
are trained for the development of the National Economy.

As a pioneer in Surveying Education and a Centre of Excellence,
our aim is to strive at producing graduates who shall be sound and
proficient in the practice of the profession.

You are welcome to explore our website for adequate information
on the activities in the School.

Prof. A. O. Akinyemi


Who We Are

Our History

Established as the first of its kind, the Federal School of Surveying, Oyo has a rich history of academic excellence and professional training.

  • Our Mission

    To produce Professionals, Technologists, and Technicians in the Field of Surveying and Geoinformatics for the National Economy in a sustainable and conducive learning environment.

  • Our Vision

    To be a pioneer in Surveying Education and Centre of Excellence, producing graduates who are sound, technically confident, and maintain high ethical standards.
